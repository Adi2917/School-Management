# Google Play listing copy

## App name

Connect Your School

## Short description

School notices, fees, results and student records in one connected portal.

## Full description

Connect Your School brings administrators, students and everyday school work into one organized portal.

Administrators can manage school-specific student records, fee status, exam results and notices. Students can securely access their own profile, fee ledger, published results and school announcements from the same connected database on web and Android.

Each account is linked to a school code so information stays inside the correct school community. Optional profile photos and school logos can be updated whenever needed, while secure PIN login keeps account credentials private.

Built and powered by BeyondNull.
